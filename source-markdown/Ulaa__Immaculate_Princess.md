===
Ulaa is Moradin's only daughter and the most extravagant member of the Morndinsamman, a goddess who holds that dwarven greatness deserves to look like dwarven greatness. She is the standard against which the finest holds measure their grandeur, her name invoked in all manner of decoration or when a bloodline is being formally recognised. She is not wasteful; she is exacting. Everything she owns is the finest available, and she considers anything less a failure of ambition.

Her realm is the Palace of the Resplendent, among the richest hills of Elysium, its walls inlaid with every gemstone her domain produces. Her petitioners spend their afterlife in conditions that would embarrass any mortal palace.
