**Corellon Larethian** (pronounced /kɒˈrɛlɒn ˌlærəˈθiːən/ kor-EL-on la-reh-THEE-an), known as the **Arch-Seldarine**, is the fey progenitor of the Seldarine pantheon and is considered the parent of all elves, for it was by their hand that the first elves wandered from Faerie. They hold court in the Crescent Grove of Arvandor, within Arborea, where the Seldarine is as fluid and changeable as a breeze or a brook, quick to anger but equally quick to forgive and forget. Anyone who has felt his mystical presence describes it as a joy like no other, followed by a deep melancholy when it fades. Those who seek art in all their work, whether magic or mundane, often worship at their altar, and loathe the Spider Queen and her priestesses for leading the drow astray.

### Commandments of Corellon Larethian
- Create, inspire, and find beauty in all that you do.
- Follow the echoes of lost magic, forgotten sites,and ancient art, for within these lie the Seldarine's first works.
- Combat the followers of the Spider Queen wherever they may be.
