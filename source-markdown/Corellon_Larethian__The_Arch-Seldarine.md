===
**Corellon Larethian** is the Ourosi deity of elves, stars, springtime, magic, reincarnation, vanity, and artistry. Known as the **Seldarine** or **Arch Heart**, Corellon is the fey progenitor of the Seldarine pantheon and the elven peoples. The Seldarine is as fluid and changeable as a breeze or a brook—quick to anger, but equally quick to forgive and forget. Anyone who has felt the mystical presence of Corellon describes it as a joy like no other, followed by a deep melancholy when his presence is no longer felt. It was by the Arch-Seldarine's hand that the first elves wandered from the Fey Realm, and for this reason, they are considered the Parent of all elves. Those who seek art in all of their work, whether magic or mundane, often worship at the altar of Corellon. They loathe the Spider Queen and her priestesses for leading the drow astray.

### Commandments of Corellon Larethian
- Create, inspire, and find beauty in all that you do.
- Follow the echoes of lost magic, forgotten sites,and ancient art, for within these lie the Seldarine's first works.
- Combat the followers of the Spider Queen wherever they may be.
