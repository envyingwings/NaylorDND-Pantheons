**Corellon Larethian** is the Ourosi deity of elves, stars, springtime, magic, reincarnation, vanity, and artistry. Known as the **Seldarine** or **Arch Heart**, Corellon is the fey progenitor of the Seldarine pantheon and the elven peoples. The Seldarine is as fluid and changeable as a breeze or a brook—quick to anger, but equally quick to forgive and forget. Anyone who has felt the mystical presence of Corellon describes it as a joy like no other, followed by a deep melancholy when his presence is no longer felt. It was by the Arch-Seldarine's hand that the first elves wandered from the Fey Realm, and for this reason, they are considered the Parent of all elves. Those who seek art in all of their work, whether magic or mundane, often worship at the altar of Corellon. They loathe the Spider Queen and her priestesses for leading the drow astray.

### Description
Most modern tapestries and tomes depict the Seldarine as a genderless elf of impossible grace and beauty, androgynous and alluring, framed by long, wavy red hair. This form is slender, long-limbed, and ageless, with features that seem to belong to every elven people at once. The Seldarine inspired many early elven art pieces, and elements of their visage or symbol are included in most elven architecture.

More esoteric accounts, however, describe a far stranger and truer shape. In these visions Corellon manifests as a great fey being made entirely of stardust and shifting light, their body a floating constellation of motes and nebulae loosely bound into an elven silhouette. The androgynous “elf” that mortals see is an ever-changing glamour, Corellon’s own radiance laboriously sculpted into a form that eyes and minds can endure. When that glamour slips, the Seldarine’s outline dissolves into whorls of color, star-fields, and aurorae—beauty too immense and fluid to be contained in flesh.

#### Celebrations and Worship Practices
The greatest common holy day of Corellon in Ouros is **Elvendawn**, corresponding roughly to midsummer. On this day, elven communities hold all-day festivals of performance and craft: plays retelling the Path of Stars and the Seldarine War, competitions of spellwork and sword-dance, and exhibitions of new art. At dusk, offerings of exceptional beauty are dedicated in silence as the first stars appear.
#### Commandments of Corellon Larethian
- Create, inspire, and find beauty in all that you do.
- Follow the echoes of lost magic, forgotten sites,and ancient art, for within these lie the Seldarine's first works.
- Combat the followers of the Spider Queen wherever they may be.
### History in Ouros
- In Ourosi myth, Corellon and the rest of the Seldarine Court began as archfey before the gods had fully settled into existence. The Court of Stars, their domain, rivalled both the Seelie and Unseelie Courts in splendor and intrigue. Unlike those courts, however, the Seldarine looked beyond the Fey Realm toward the wider multiverse.
- Elven apocrypha speaks of the **Path of Stars**, a legendary road of constellations carved by the Seldarine Court through the firmament. Walking this path, Corellon and their closest companions abandoned the Feywild’s endless cycle and ascended to true divinity, claiming the realm of **Arvandor** as their own. From there, they began to shape and shepherd their elven children across worlds. In Ourosi theology, this choice marks the moment Corellon becomes not merely an archfey, but the Seldarine—a god who remembers the wild court and yet chooses responsibility for an entire people.
- Aurashnee—once Corellon’s consort and the Seldarine’s weaver of destiny, fell to a growing paranoia with no clear origin. Later Seldarine lore claims an Abyssal Lord sought to corrupt her mind in order to seize the artifact Amarth’lóm she guarded, planting in her suspicions to inspire treachery. In this state, Aurashnee attempted to overthrow Corellon and seize leadership of the Seldarine, sparking the brief but devastating Seldarine War. Defeated, she reatreated into the Abyss, she was transformed into Lolth, losing the Amarth’lóm in her descent; the Seldarine concealed the Loom in safekeeping should she ever be cured of her corruption. Her chosen elves followed her into exile and became the wicked caste of drow, among which Corellon’s son Vhaeraun, who also inspired Eilistraee to leave, though she did not follow Lolth into the Abyss.
- When the Seldarine Court was disbanded to form the Seldarine Pantheon, many eladrin chose to leave the Feywild and journey across the multiverse. These first wanderers followed starlit ways laid by Corellon’s hand, traversing Inner Planes such as Air and Water, the glooms of the Shadowfell, and finally the Material Plane. Only a select few were permitted to accompany the Seldarine directly to Arvandor, where they became shining archons among the elves.
    - Those who settled in mortal realms adapted to their new environments and, over ages, gave rise to the many elven kindreds: Avariel, Aquatic Elves, High Elves (often called Common Eladrin), Wood Elves, Lythari, and a host of more elven subraces. In every telling, it is Corellon’s mutability that allows their children to change so dramatically without losing the essential elven spark.
