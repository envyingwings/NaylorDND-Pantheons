===
Tharmekhûl is the eldest of the All-Hammer's children, the first Azer shaped at his father's hands with the singular purpose of tending the Soul Forge and keeping it fit for the work of making worlds and dwarves. He has held that position since before the Morndinsamman had a name and shows no interest in any other. While his father is occupied with the larger work, Tharmekhûl maintains the equipment of the clan, and seeds geothermal vents across the planes of the multiverse to supply the heat the Soul Forge demands. He is meticulous and incredibly proud of his work to the point of being difficult about it.

His realm is the Istor's Forge in Dwarfhome, the beating heart of the dwarven afterlife, which dwarven scholars hold to be the source of all heat in its surrounding plane. His petitioners tend it alongside him, and ensure no fire in Dwarfhome goes out without his express orders.
