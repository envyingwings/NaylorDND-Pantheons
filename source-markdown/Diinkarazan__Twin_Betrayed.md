===
Diinkarazan is the twin of the derro's patron god, betrayed by him and left to have his mind consumed by Ilsensine while his sibling fled with stolen magic. He has no organised worship; the derro shamans mention him only to praise his brother's cunning escape at his expense, and whatever faithful he retains he holds through misdirection and the pull of his madness rather than any doctrine or covenant. He is released from his prison once every fifty years for a single day and spends it hunting derro communities in revenge for their abandonment of him.

The Prison of the Mad God is not a true layer of the Abyss but a plane torn between Carceri and Pandemonium, its imprisoning and maddening qualities balanced so precisely between the two that it cannot slip into either, leaving it suspended in the Abyss instead. At its centre is a gem-set throne within a perpetual vortex of wind, gas, and orbiting stone, to which Diinkarazan is bound.
