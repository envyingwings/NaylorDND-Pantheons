===
**Kord**, better known as **the Stormlord**, is the Ourosi god of the sky, storms, battle, health, victory, and heroic contest. Also called the Stormbringer and Dragonslayer God, he is revered by warriors, sailors, gladiators, and athletes across the world, and honored by giantkind of the Ourosi faith. Reveling in all tests of strength, the blessing of the Stormlord finds those that prove themselves on the battlefield. Worshiped by athletes and warriors all across Ouros, he exalts those whose force of spirit and passion for victory call his attention. He brings tumultuous storms over land and sea, and those who wish for clearer skies offer their praises and prayers to appease him.

### Description
Within his temples and carvings, the Stormlord is shown as a quintessential giant warrior: enormously tall, broad-shouldered, bare-chested or nude, with a full beard and short, curling hair. His body is a study in power—corded muscle, scarred skin, and a relaxed but ready stance that suggests he might leap into combat at any second. Many depictions, especially in giantish shrines, emphasize his physical perfection to the point of idealization, presenting him as the living archetype of strength and vitality. [TV Tropes+1](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/CriticalRoleGods?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

In most giant and Ordning-inspired art, Kord is shown standing over or wrestling a dragon, often with one hand at the wyrm’s throat while the other brandishes or rests upon a greatsword forged of lightning. The blade itself is frequently stylized as a bolt driven through the dragon’s skull, reinforcing his epithet Wyrmgared—“Dragonslayer.” In human and smaller-folk iconography, these scenes are sometimes scaled down: Kord appears as a towering human or half-giant, wrestling sea-serpents, storm elementals, or titanic beasts upon storm-lashed cliffs.

Sailors paint his symbol on their sails as four-pronged lightning radiating from a shield, while gladiatorial arenas and athletic colleges sometimes display murals of the Stormbringer striding naked into a storm, laughing as thunder crashes overhead. In visions, witnesses speak of a looming silhouette wreathed in cloud and a booming laugh riding the thunder, or of a hand seizing a bolt of lightning out of the sky and hurling it point-first into the sea.

#### Celebrations and Worship Practices
Kord’s primary holy day is the Day of Challenging: a festival of contests held on the seventh day of the month of Twouary. On this day, communities host public bouts, tests of endurance, footraces, wrestling matches, and feats of strength. Oaths are sworn, grudges are settled in the ring instead of on the street, and champions are crowned in Kord’s name. Sudden storms during the festival are taken as a sign of the Stormlord’s keen interest. 
#### Commandments of Kord
- Bravery above all. There is no glory in cowardice.
- Strength is the path to greatness, but greatness is the responsible use of strength.
- The glory of the Stormlord lives through your own glory on the battlefield.
### History in Ouros
- In giant mythology, Kord began as a storm giant hero who swore to cleanse the skies and mountains of dragonkind during the first great war between giants and dragons—two early children of the Primordials. He bathed himself in the mingled blood of every dragon he slew, and in doing so crossed some threshold between mortal and divine. In the oldest Ordning tales, he is called **Wyrmgared** (“Dragonslayer”) and is not named among the elder gods of the Ordning, but honored as Annam’s exalted right hand: an exarch, weapon, and champion whose battles helped secure giant supremacy in that mythic age.
- Kord is said to judge mortals by how they face adversity. A clear sky is not a blessing in his eyes but a lull; the real test is how one stands when the wind howls, the ship lists, or the battlefield turns against them. Myths tell of champions who cursed Kord for sending storms, only to later thank him when those same storms hardened them enough to survive calamities that would have killed softer souls.
### Appendix
#### The Ordning — Giant Pantheon
The Stormwarrior himself is not a god within the Ordining of Giants, but is an exarch of its king, Annam All-Father, the Lord of the Ordning. The Ordning is a group of deities worshipped by giants and giant-kin.
