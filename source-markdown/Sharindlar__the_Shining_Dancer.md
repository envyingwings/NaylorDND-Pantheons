===
Sharindlar is the Shining Dancer, the most warmly regarded member of the Morndinsamman, whose goodwill extends further than any of her kin and whose realm serves as the neutral ground where the clan's most difficult relationships are brokered. She is spontaneous and exuberant in a pantheon that is largely neither, given to bursts of wild laughter and impromptu dancing, and has managed to establish working relationships with members of the clan that her peers have written off entirely. She holds no grudges and nurses no rivalries, which makes her uniquely suited to serve as her Moradin's ambassador when communication with the estranged members of the family becomes unavoidable.

Her realm is the Merciful Court, a circle of standing stones on a foothill where nightly dances are held without exception. It is the one place in the dwarven afterlife where the estranged and the exiled are permitted to be in the Morndinsamman's presence.
