The Gemheart asks only this of the world: that it not take itself too seriously. Garl Glittergold is the laughing patron of invention and celebration, a golden-skinned eccentric who breathed the first gnomes into life from gems plucked off a cavern wall, then led them into the wider world by telling them a very good joke. That his children have repaid him by becoming the most curious, inventive, and joyful people in creation is a source of boundless pride. His clerics and followers, gnomes and otherwise, understand that wealth is not only gold, but the richness of a life well-lived: a clever solution, a room full of laughter, a thing made beautifully. Garl is accompanied always by Arumdina the Justifier, a sentient sapphire battleaxe who is widely considered the more sensible of the two.

Garl wanders freely throughout the Golden Hills, a seven-peaked realm that glows with warm light on the plane of Bytopia, though his own hill of Glitterhome sits at its heart. He is rarely found there.

### Description
Gnomish art traditionally portrays the Gemheart as a jovial, gold-skinned gnome with gemstone-bright eyes. He wears a many-pocketed coat filled with trinkets, snacks, and enchanted odds-and-ends, and adorns himself with glimmering jewelry of his own make. His favored weapon is a two-faced battleaxe—one side for carving stone and metal, the other for warding off threats to his people. Among gnomes, the axe symbolizes not violence, but craftsmanship and protection.

#### Celebrations and Worship Practices
The church of Garl is highly regarded in gnome society, often serving as both spiritual and civic leadership. Rites tend to be lively, full of illusions, music, and theatrics—designed to keep people laughing and learning rather than bored and passive.
#### Commandments of Garl Glittergold
- Seek wonder in every cavern, forest, and corner of the world.
- Never take yourself too seriously, lest you lose touch with those you protect and care for.
- Do not fear change or the unorthodox — therein lies the future.
### History in Ouros
- Gnomish myth claims Garl Glittergold placed gemstones throughout the caverns he explored on every world he visited, using them as markers to track where his wandering feet had already roamed. Caverns where every wall and ceiling glimmer with such stones are known as **Glittercaves**, sacred sites where gnomes believe their prayers reach the Merry Fool most clearly.
- In one such cavern beneath an ancient battleground, Garl found droplets of Corellon’s blood dripping from stalactites, spilled during a clash with Gruumsh, the Ruiner. Curious, the Gemheart placed gemstones across the cavern floor to see what magic might form when Archfey blood mingled with gemstone. Upon returning from his surface wanderings, he discovered small folk newly born from that mixture—tiny, nimble beings with hair the colors of the gemstones he had left. These were the first **gnomes**. Garl taught them humor, curiosity, craftsmanship, and the joy of community, encouraging them to explore the world above and below and shape their own destinies with sharp wit and clever hands.

## Known Organizations
- **Three-Hundred Sibyls.** The infamous order of mystics and diviners of Sibylline, the Three-Hundred Sibyls pay tribute to Garl Glittergold, Ioun, and Sardior in their operations, which entail the delving into distant worlds and environments to uncover the innovations of others and return them to Ouros. The Three-Hundred Sibyls and their observatory were Ouros’s source of information about the Dawn War as it was happening, as the gods did not walk or fight upon its surface, and did not return until they declared their departure and the installment of the Divine Gate.
