### Description
```columns
id: -Bahamut-Descrition
===
The Platinum Dragon is often seen emblazoned on shields and armor, both functional and decorative, in the form of a brilliant dragon head in profile. Temples and works of art depict a massive, glittering dragon with vibrant platinum scales and a seemingly endless wingspan. His eyes are commonly rendered as deep blue or icy indigo, said to shift in hue with his mood.

Draconic scripture insists that in his true form he is the only platinum dragon in existence, long and sinuous, his scales shining like mirrored starlight. In mortal lands, however, he is known to walk veiled: as a weary but clear-eyed elder in simple robes, as a traveling knight in immaculate platinum plate, or as a faceless figure cloaked in silver-white, accompanied by seven golden canaries that are, in truth, ancient gold dragons in disguise.
```

#### Celebrations and Worship Practices
Bahamut’s worship favors deeds over words. His faithful are encouraged to live their devotion through acts of courage, protection, and fair judgment, rather than elaborate liturgies.

In the eras before the Calamity and the rise of the Platinum Sanctuary, temples to the Platinum Dragon were rare and austere. Those few that existed were beautiful yet restrained edifices: clean lines of pale stone, minimal ornament beyond the dragon-head sigil, and quiet cloisters for meditation and recuperation. In the current age, when many think of a Temple of the Platinum Dragon, they imagine those founded or sanctioned by the Platinum Sanctuary. These temples are far more opulent and unmistakably draconic in influence. Though lavish in stone and metal, these temples are expected to keep their wealth in motion: a hoard that does not serve justice is considered spiritually tarnished. The Platinum Sanctuary enforces strict audits to ensure offerings are used to uplift the vulnerable, not indulge the clergy.

Bahamut’s primary holy day, Embertide. On this day, temples hold solemn trials reviewing the conduct of local knights, judges, and rulers. Confessions are encouraged; penitent wrongdoers may receive quests of atonement rather than immediate punishment. Communities light great braziers or bonfires at dusk, symbolizing the dragon’s breath scouring away corruption while warming the righteous.
#### Commandments of Bahamut
- Stand as a paragon of honor and justice.
- Smite evil wherever it is found, yet show compassion to those who have strayed from righteousness.
- Defend the weak, bring freedom to the persecuted, and protect the ideals of justice and order.
### History in Ouros
- Draconic myths claim that Bahamut and Tiamat were born from the sundered corpse of the Primordial Dragon-God, torn apart during the first war between dragons and giants at the dawn of the Material Plane. From the Primordial’s breath and bones arose Bahamut, from its wrathful heart and venom sprang Tiamat, and from the scattered fragments of mind and blood coalesced Sardior, their younger brother and the first of the gem dragons.
- From their first awakening, Bahamut and Tiamat stood opposed. Where Bahamut sought order, protection, and a just peace for dragonkind and mortals alike, Tiamat desired dominion, hoard, and fear. Many legends tell of early ages in which chromatic dragons ravaged the newborn world, only to be driven back or redeemed by Bahamut’s champions. Their war—ideological as much as physical—continues through every age of Ouros.
- During the Dawn War that ended the gods’ open rule over Ouros, Bahamut stood among the foremost of the Prime Deities. He led metallic dragons and mortal knights against the hosts of the Betrayer Gods, clashing again and again with Tiamat, whose chromatic legions assailed cities and strongholds under his protection. Tales from this era speak of the Platinum Dragon descending in blinding radiance to shatter infernal war machines and of him sharing council-fire with Moradin and Kord as they prepared the last great counteroffensives of the war.

## Known Organizations

Bahamut’s faith often expresses itself through knightly orders and disciplined fraternities as much as through temples.

- **Questers of the Platinum Flame.** A holy order of wandering knights-errant and companions dedicated to seeking out great evils and confronting them wherever they take root. They are guided by a brilliant blue flame first ignited within a platinum brazier, supposedly by Bahamut’s breath; and the flame glows brighter and flickers towards growing evil, and the Questers follow its direction.
- **The Platinum Sanctuary.** Grand cathedrals and fortified sanctuaries, serving as regional hearts of Bahamut’s worship. Within their walls, knights train, refugees are sheltered, alms given, and campaigns against great evils are planned. When many think of a given Temple of the Platinum Dragon, it is typically one such built and staffed by clergy trained by the Platinum Sanctuary.
