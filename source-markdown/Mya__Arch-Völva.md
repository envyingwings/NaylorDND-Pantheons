===
Mya is the Voice of Wisdom, the goddess to whom dwarves turn when the question is not what to build or how to fight but what to remember and what to pass on. Dwarven myth holds that Moradin sought her counsel before breathing souls into the elemental Azer he had shaped from fire and stone, and that it was Mya who told him what a mortal people would need to survive across generations. The proliferation of twins and triplets among dwarves is attributed to her hand, the first gift she gave to a people whose survival would depend on the bonds between those who shared a womb.

Her realm is the Hearth Eternal, a vast longhouse of warm firelight with every wall inscribed with the sagas of dwarven bloodlines stretching back to the first generation. Her petitioners spend eternity ensuring that nothing a dwarf ever lived and learned is lost.
