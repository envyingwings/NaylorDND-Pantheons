===
Thard Harr is the most feral member of the Morndinsamman, a god who communicates through animal calls and physical gestures rather than speech, and who is emotionally and geographically estranged from the rest of the clan despite never being in poor standing with them. He is the patron of wild dwarves specifically, those who abandoned the holds for the deep jungles and built a way of life entirely around survival and instinct rather than craft and tradition. He lives with the beasts of the jungle as kin rather than presiding over them, and responds to threats against his people with the same ferocity. Commonly attributed to him is the fey mood, the involuntary creative seizure that occasionally overtakes dwarves of any background and produces work far beyond their natural skill.

His realm is the Forbidden Plateau in Krigala, a heavily forested highland swarming with dinosaurs and beasts of every kind. He rarely stays there, preferring to roam all three layers of the Beastlands as one of its animals rather than preside over his realm as a god.
