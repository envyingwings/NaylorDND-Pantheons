===
Roknar is the Lord with Two Tongues, a god who has never done more than the bare minimum required to keep his seat at the clan table. He inserts himself into the plans of others when it serves him, lending just enough assistance to remain indispensable while ensuring that anything which might inconvenience him quietly fails from within. He is not idle out of weakness but out of a profound conviction that effort spent on anyone else's behalf is effort wasted. The Morndinsamman finds him more useful within reach than beyond it, doing just enough in their shared interests to protect him from exile.

His realm is Hollowhoard, where nothing is demanded from its inhabitants yet nothing is offered either. Those who arrive find themselves sitting with exactly what they gave and what they earned across their lives, which in his domain always amounts to less than they believed they deserved.
