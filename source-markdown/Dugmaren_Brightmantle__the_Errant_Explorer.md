===
Dugmaren Brightmantle is the most chaotic of the Morndinsamman, a divine embodiment of the industrial progressivism and exploratory streak that dwarven culture carries beneath its conservatism. He pursues knowledge regardless of its utility, abandons projects the moment something more interesting presents itself, and has ruined things he was not supposed to touch more times than any of his kin care to count. He is tolerated by even the most lawful members of the clan because his inventions, however haphazardly arrived at, have proven their worth often enough to justify the chaos that surrounds him.

His realm is Soot Hall, caverns coated in millennia of forge-ash, filled with furnaces, workshops, and libraries of esoteric lore, and never quiet for he grants his petitioners the ability to work without need for sleep.
