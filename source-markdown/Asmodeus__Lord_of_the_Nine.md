===
**Asmodeus**, known across Ouros as the **Lord of the Nine**, the **Lord of Lies**, and the **King of Baator**, is the god of the Hells, devils, indulgence, sin, contracts, and domination. The first and greatest of the archdevils, he reigns over the Nine Hells from his citadel in Nessus, ruling with total and unquestioned authority. A master of temptation and tyrannical order, he embodies lawful evil in its most absolute expression—binding mortals and immortals alike through oaths, bargains, and forbidden pleasures. His worshippers include devils, corrupt officials, decadent aristocrats, and warlocks who believe their ascent to power lies through the structure of dominance he embodies.

### Commandments of Asmodeus
- Assert dominance and power over others. Show your strength of will in the image of your Lord.
- Repay cruelty done unto you with further evil. If others show you kindness, exploit it.
- As you ascend to power, do not pity or show mercy to those you climb over to get there. The weak do not warrant your compassion—compassion itself is a weakness.

- **[[Baalzebul — Lord of the Flies|Baalzebul, Archduke of Maladomini]]**. Once a shining prince, now a bloated lord of decay and failed grandeur. His ruined palaces and abandoned projects serve as warnings of pride that refuses to accept imperfection.
- **[[Dispater — Iron Archduke of Dis|Dispater, Archduke of Dis]].** Patron of paranoia, surveillance, and iron-walled cities. He rules from the Iron Tower of Dis, an ever-expanding labyrinth of forges, prisons, and archives that model the most oppressive bureaucracies of Ouros.
- **[[Fierna — Lady of Phlegethos|Fierna, Archduchess of Phlegethos]].** The mistress of passion inflamed into cruelty, ruling a sea of living flame where pleasure and pain are indistinguishable tools of control.
- **[[Glasya — Princess of Malbolge|Glasya, Archduchess of Malbolge]].** Daughter of Asmodeus and mistress of intrigue, rebellion, and “reform” through crime. Her layer is a shifting maze of courts, slums, and gallows, reflecting the corruption of mortal cities.
- **[[Levistus — Prince of Stygia|Levistus, Archduke of Stygia]]**. A traitor entombed alive within a floating glacier, patron of treachery suspended but never forgiven. His frozen realm attracts those who seek to escape consequences by binding them in time.
- **[[Mammon — Viscount of Minauros|Mammon, Archduke of Minauros]].** The serpent-coiled lord of avarice and corruption, whose sinking marsh-city drowns those who mistake wealth for worth. He is invoked by embezzlers, usurers, and those who trade lives for coin.
- **[[Mephistopheles — Brimstone Baron|Mephistopheles, Archduke of Cania]]**. Master of infernal arcana and experimental sorceries, ruling a plane of impossible cold. He tempts mages with forbidden formulae and offers empires new weapons in exchange for concessions of law and soul.
- **[[Zariel — Archduchess of Avernus|Zariel, Archduchess of Avernus]]**. Once a fiery angel of war, who in a fit of rage descended into the hells and slaughtered legions of Hell alone, and now the infernal marshal of crusades in the Blood War and bargains made for martial glory. Her layer is a battlefield-scoured wasteland where armies of devils and mortals alike march in endless campaigns.
