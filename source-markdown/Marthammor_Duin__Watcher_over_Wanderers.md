===
Marthammor Duin is the Finder-of-Trails, the most openly curious member of the Morndinsamman, whose interest lies in the world beyond the clanholds of dwarvenkind. He watches over dwarves who have left their holds to live or travel among other people, and is the god most likely to be invoked by a dwarf far from home.

His realm is the Cavern of Rest beneath Nidavellir, guarded by the souls of dwarves who died travelling far from home and by war dogs and boars he trained himself. Very rarely is he found within his realm, instead following whatever paths throughout the planes he has not yet walked.
