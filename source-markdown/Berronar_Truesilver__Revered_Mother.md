===
Berronar Truesilver is the matriarch of the Morndinsamman, whose marriage to the All-Hammer formed the pantheon as it exists today by uniting their two families into a single clan. All values of dwarvenkind trace back to the household she and her husband established at the founding of the pantheon. Dwarven culture does not merely worship Berronar but is modelled on her governance. Every clan hall, every sworn oath, every stronghold and its history reflects the household she founded.

Her realm is Dwarfhome, shared with her Moradin. It is the destination of dwarves who have lived well by the standards of the clan: those who kept their oaths, maintained a welcome home, and upheld their obligations to kin. For the dwarven faithful it represents not reward so much as homecoming, a final return to the household from which all dwarven life descends.
