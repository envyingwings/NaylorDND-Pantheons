===
Gendwar Argrim is the Doomed Dwarf, the god called upon when a dwarf knows their time is short and their work is not yet finished. He asks nothing of those who pray to him except that they spend whatever remains of themselves on what matters, and his answer to prayers is not the promise of survival but just enough time to achieve their goals. He is not a god of death or battle specifically, but of the unfinished and the urgent, invoked by warriors in losing fights and elders in failing health alike.

His realm is the Final Hall, a fortress deep in Abellio whose gates are always open and whose inhabitants are always working. Nothing in it is complete. Every project is mid-construction, every campaign mid-march, every song unfinished. His petitioners do not rest there; they continue.

### Commandments of Gendwar Argrim
- The only hope the dwarven race has to survive is if its enemies are utterly destroyed. Focus on their destruction. They breed faster than the True Folk and spawn ten times our numbers within one dwarven generation.
- Honour, glory, wealth, and love are all meaningless and trivial while the future is at stake.
- Expect to die in battle, but make your death cost a thousand enemies their own. If the dwarves survive only in song and legend, ours will be an empty legacy.
