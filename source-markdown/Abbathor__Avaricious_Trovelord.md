===
Abbathor is sometimes known as the Wyrm of Avarice, for the Trovelord has such a lust for minerals and metals that it is comparible to the hoarding nature of dragonkind. Abbathor's power and wealth is said to be the reason as to why dwarves are so susceptible to the curse of Dragon Sickness. He surrounds himself with self-interested misers and thieves and lifts from their treasures and feeds them his own to transform them into wyverns and drakes to protect his temples and vaults like guard dogs, giving them the reputation of the most well-guarded treasuries in the multiverse. His position in the Morndinsamman is to protect the treasures of his clan, which the other gods frequently have to force him into submission to relinquish when they are needed.

His realm is Glitterhell, a vault of immeasurable wealth so heavy it has sunk into Oinos in the Grey Wastes of Hades. The gold there cannot be spent and the gems cannot be moved for Abbathor refuses to give up even a fraction of his wealth; the faithful of Abbathor or those damned for their greed who end up there spend eternity surrounded by everything they ever wanted and unable to touch any of it.
