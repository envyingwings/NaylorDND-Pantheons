Ioun (pronounced: /aɪˈuːn/ eye-OON), the Knowing Mentor, is the Ourosi goddess of knowledge, truth, prophecy, divination, and skill. Also known as the Knowing Mistress and the Cobalt Prophet, she is revered by sages, seers, archivists, and tacticians, as well as any who live by wit and learning rather than strength of arm. Revered by seers, sages, and teachers of all walks of life, the Knowing Mentor guided the growth of civilization throughout the ages like sunlight guides the branches of a tree. Her teachings were silenced when she was grievously wounded by the Chained Oblivion during the Calamity, and though she survived and went into hiding behind the Divine Gate, her followers are hunted by agents of her ancient foes to this day; but Truth and Knowledge cannot be quelled so easily.

### Description
Common representations show the Knowing Mentor as a greying, cobalt-skinned mature woman with a welcoming, matronly smile, swathed in billowing robes and scarves that fan out into shelves of books and scrolls. Some call her the Knowing Mistress and imagine her as a divine headmistress, overseeing the education of gods and mortals alike.

In more esoteric iconography, her lower body dissolves into constellations and whirling star-maps, her robe’s hem trailing strings of glowing “stones” that circle her like miniature moons—the likely origin of the Ioun Stones that bear her name. In visions reported by her most devout, her face sometimes fractures into three overlapping visages, each crowned with an open eye, speaking past and present and possible future in a braided chorus.

#### Celebrations and Worship Practices
Public temples to Ioun were once common, but many were destroyed or repurposed after her wounding. In the current age, her worship often survives in quiet corners of universities, star-observatories, private libraries, or monastic orders whose true allegiance is recorded only in coded sigils of three open eyes.

Ioun has no grand, worldwide holy day; her faith teaches that every day spent in honest study, teaching, or truth-telling is itself sacred. In some Ourosi traditions, however, the anniversary of the Calamity is marked by a day-long fast from speech, broken at nightfall by a shared reading of recovered or new and untold stories and texts.
#### Commandments of Ioun
- Unmask those who would destroy the Knowing Mentor. Learn their secrets and unveil them to the world.
- Uphold and teach the importance of reason, perception, and truth in guiding one's emotions and path.
- Condemn those who lie without moral cause, for evil folk gain power when their followers obscure the truth. Never stoop to the level of selfish liars.

## Known Organizations
- **The Three-Hundred Sibyls.** A council of oracular mystics based in Sibylline, who honor Garl, Sardior, and Ioun jointly. Where they work in her name, they focus on interpreting world-spanning events and warning of threats to knowledge itself.
- **The Cobalt Soul.** A monastic order founded in the wake of the Calamity, devoted to the recovery of lost knowledge and the exposure of lies. Members blend martial discipline with rigorous study, believing that ignorance is the root of tyranny. Many of their monasteries operate as public archives and universities by day and intelligence networks by night.
## Divine Relics
- **Ioun Stones.** Not commonly recognised as such divine relics as rare enchanted gems from an earlier era, these multifaceted crystals and motes of mineral light orbit the heads of those blessed by Ioun, granting insight, memory, and other boons. Some traditions claim they grow naturally in the deep planes of mineral and starlight; others insist they are shards of her own ever-turning thought, broken off and gifted to mortals.
