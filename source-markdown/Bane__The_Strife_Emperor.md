**Bane** is the Ourosi god of conflict, discipline, strategy, warfare, and oppression. Known as the **Strife Emperor** and the **Black Hand**, he is the patron of despots, warlords, drill-sergeants, and blood-drenched conquerors who seek to bend the world beneath ordered violence. Blood-drenched armies, brutal empires, and many goblinoid tribes worship him as they strike out to break “lesser peoples” and grind them into disciplined servitude. From his dread realm of Banehold, he acts indirectly through his priests, champions, and goblinoid demigods, pursuing a single, unchanging goal: total domination under his iron will.

### Description
The Strife Emperor is often depicted as a brutish, ogre-like man clad from head to toe in jagged black armor. Heads dangle from his belt, and the shadows that obscure his helmeted face do not hide his unblinking yellow eyes.

Those who long for conquest often see their own people as superior to all others. Thus, somewhat appropriately, depictions of the Strife Emperor are contentious and myriad: he is usually portrayed as a member of whatever race currently bears his banner. Hobgoblin mosaics show him as a towering general with blazing eyes, human iconography as a dark emperor wreathed in banners and shackles, and orcish cults sometimes depict him as a massive, scar-slashed warlord.

#### Celebrations and Worship Practices
Among the evil gods, the cults of the Black Hand are some of the most stable, disciplined, and enduring. Where many dark faiths tear themselves apart through infighting, Bane’s worship enforces a ruthless hierarchy that mirrors a military chain of command: every priest is a commander to those beneath them and a weapon in the hand of those above.

Public worship of the Strife Emperor is illegal in many civilized realms of Ouros, alongside that of other Betrayer Gods. Yet wherever there are barracks and prisons, there are often hidden shrines: a gauntleted fist carved under a desk, a chain worked subtly into a banner, or a black altar in a fortress’s deepest cellar.
#### Commandments of Bane
- Fear is your ally. Conquer your fear, and inspire it in your foes.
- Disorder and rebellion are to be punished severely.
- To kill is life's greatest pleasure, and perfecting the art of slaughter is life's greatest pursuit.
### History in Ouros
- Goblinoid legends speak of a time when their gods were many and squabbling, until Maglubiyet, the Mighty One, rose to rule them with an iron fist. In Ouros, those tales are often retold with Bane looming over even Maglubiyet, the Black Hand upon the High Chieftain’s shoulder, guiding every cull, sacrifice, and campaign.

Maglubiyet, the Blue Chieftain; and all others of the Goblinoid Pantheon pay tribute to the Strife Emperor.
- **[[Maglubiyet — Blue Chieftain|Maglubiyet, the Blue Chieftain]]** High Chieftain of the goblinoid gods and Bane’s favored general, Maglubiyet rules goblins and hobgoblins with an iron fist, urging constant war and strict internal hierarchy. Under Bane’s shadow, he drives goblinoid hordes to prove their worth in endless campaigns.
- **[[Hruggek — the Dropbear|Hruggek, the Dropbear]].** Chief deity of bugbears and exarch of Bane, Hruggek embodies ambush, violence, and murder. His priests decorate their halls with severed heads and serve as shock-commanders in Banite armies, sowing terror ahead of the main advance. 
- **[[Grankhul — the Hunterbug|Grankhul, the Hunterbug]].** Bugbear god of stealth, hunting, and surprise, Grankhul teaches that the most perfect kill is one the victim never sees coming. As a lesser exarch, he lends the Strife Emperor elite scouts, assassins, and spies hidden behind the front lines.
- **[[Khurgorbaeyag — the Overseer|Khurgorbaeyag, the Red Overseer]]].** Goblin god of slavery, oppression, and rigid social order. Khurgorbaeyag oversees slave-takings and camp discipline in Bane’s name, ensuring that every collar tightens the Black Hand’s grip.
- [[Bargrivyek — Indurate Enforcer|Bargrivyek, Peace's Enforcer]]. A god of goblinoid cooperation and unity, Bargrivyek keeps goblin and hobgoblin tribes pointed outward, making sure their aggression is directed toward expanding empires rather than internal feuds. He is no pacifist—his “peace” is one earned by quelling all dissenting voices.
- **[[Nomog-Geaya — the Red General|Nomog-Geaya, the Red General]].** Hobgoblin deity of war and authoritarian rule, Nomog-Geaya exemplifies brutal discipline, stoicism, and cold-blooded efficiency. As Maglubiyet’s chief lieutenant and a loyal exarch of Bane, he commands elite hobgoblin legions and embodies the ideal Banite officer: unquestioning, ruthless, and utterly devoted to victory and order.
- **[[Skiggaret — Bugbear’s Bogeyman|Skiggaret, Bugbear's Bogeyman]].** The goblinoid bogeyman, and half-mad bugbear god of fear, darkness, and insanity, Skiggaret serves as Bane’s living weaponized terror against his own goblinoid troops. Among bugbears he is more often placated than worshipped; his sudden manifestations drive tribes to frenzied violence or flight.
