**Erathis**, also known as **The Lawbearer** or the **Cobalt Lady**, is the goddess of civilization, law, peace, commerce, and progress. A patron of judges, lawmakers, city-builders, and inventors, she represents the order and structure that allows societies to flourish. The driving inspiration behind many great inventions, the creation of vast cities, and law and order within society, the Lawbearer claims dominion over civilization. Judges and lawful rulers pay respect at her temples, which are central structures in major cities around the world. Peace and order, through structure and law, guide the will of her devout followers. The Lawbearer has a tempestuous romance with the [[Melora, The Wildmother|Wildmother]], a furious love that is only tempered when civilization and nature are in balance. Civil unrest and riots blossom within her beloved cities in these times of imbalance.

#### Commandments of Erathis
- Utilize the company and aid of others. The efforts of the individual often pale against the capabilities of community.
- Strive to tame the wilds in the name of civilization,and defend the points of light and order against the chaos of darkness.

## Acolyte of Asmodeus

_Variant Acolyte_
You have given your devotion to the Lord of the Nine — Asmodeus, the King of Baator, Greater God of Indulgence, Sin, Damnation, Politics, and Negotiation. His is not a faith of ecstasy or comfort. It is a faith of clarity: that desire is the engine of all mortal action, that law exists to be defined by those strong enough to define it, and that every soul has its price. You learned his rites in hushed administrative chambers and candlelit vestries, amid ledgers and contracts, among clergy who dress like magistrates and speak like lawyers. To serve the Lord of the Nine is not to be wicked — it is to be honest about what the world is, and patient enough to profit from it.

**Skill Proficiencies:** Deception, Religion 
**Languages:** Any two (Typically Celestial and Infernal)
