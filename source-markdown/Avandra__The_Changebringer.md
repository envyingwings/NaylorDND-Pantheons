### Description
She is often depicted as a young woman, usually human or halfling, of dark complexion and long, light brown or golden hair that cascades to form the road left behind her. Most art shows her in constant motion, ever leading into the unknown, with her hair unfurling into winding paths, caravan tracks, and city streets.

Her garments are practical yet flowing—traveler’s leathers half-hidden under a cloak or dress caught in an unseen wind, often trimmed in spring greens and gilded golds. In some halfling art, she bears a satchel of coins and seeds, scattering both as she walks.

#### Celebrations and Worship Practices
The Changebringer’s worship is informal and lively. Her shrines are small stone cairns, painted waystones, or simple coins nailed to trees and posts at crossroads. Wayside chapels to Avandra are built with wide doors, battered benches, and a spare pallet or two—any traveler is welcome to rest, share gossip, and move on lighter than they arrived.

The Changebringer's holy day, called New Dawn, takes place on the first day of the first month, when the old year gives way to the new. On this day, communities gather at high places, city walls, shorelines, or crossroads to watch the last sunset of the old year and the first sunrise of the new, eating, drinking, and speaking openly of what they hope to change. 
#### Commandments of Avandra
- Luck favors the bold. Your fate is your own to grasp, and to do so is to have the Changebringer behind you.
- Change is inevitable. The righteous can ensure that such change is for the better.
- Rise up against tyranny. Fight for the freedom of yourself and others when you can, and inspire others to fight when you cannot.
### History in Ouros
- Ourosi tales say that before she was the Changebringer, Avandra was only “the Wanderling,” a young divine spirit who could not bear to stay in one place. She walked from realm to realm until she collapsed, exhausted and hungry, upon the borders of the Green Fields. Yondalla found her there and took her in, letting the Wanderling share in the endless bread and stew of the halfling paradise. The Hearthdame would raise the Wanderling as her own fostered daughter, better preparing her for the wanderlust that will drive her away in her next bout of strength.

## Known Organizations

- **Wayfarer’s Fellowship.** A loose brotherhood of caravan guards, scouts, and guides who swear to never strand a traveler without cause. They keep rough maps, tales of safe wells and dangerous passes, and maintain roadside shrines to Avandra.
- **Horizon Sisters.** A semi-monastic order of predominantly women and nonbinary devotees who take vows of the road. They serve as mediators in border disputes, escorts for refugees and freed slaves, and pathfinders and heralds of new routes that bypass exploitative tolls and blockades. 

## Divine Relics

- **Kiss of the Changebringer.** A beautiful emerald set at the heart of a silver chain, humming with contained possibility. In legend, the Kiss grants its bearer impossible escapes, sudden turns of luck, and the power to rewrite the terms of a bargain at the last possible moment. It is said to awaken fully only in times of great upheaval, when one bold choice could change the fate of nations. It is said to have been stolen in legend by Asmodeus, when he ripped it from the Changebringer’s neck and locked it away within a vault hidden upon the Material Plane.
