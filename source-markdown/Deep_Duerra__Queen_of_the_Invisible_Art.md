===
Deep Duerra is a hero-goddess who took mortal form to steal the secrets of illithid psionics from the mind flayers who enslaved her people, and led the duergar rebellion that broke that enslavement. The psionic power the duergar carry is her inheritance to them, seized rather than gifted, and she remains the patron of their ongoing conquest of the Underdark. She sits among the exiled members of the Morndinsamman, cast out alongside her predecessor for doctrines the clan would not sanction, and rules her following from beyond the reach of the family that disowned her.

Her realm is the Citadel of Thought, a fortress of psionic architecture built into one of the hollow iron cubes that drift through Thuldanin. It is where her faithful continue the conquest she began in life, and where the psionic secrets she stole from the illithids are studied and expanded without limit.
